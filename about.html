Overview

A technology career spanning software quality engineering, test automation, systems engineering, enterprise platform validation, and technical leadership.

Satish Reddy Budati is a technology professional with more than 18 years of experience across software quality engineering, test automation, systems engineering, enterprise platform validation, and technical leadership.

His work has focused on improving the quality, reliability, and operational confidence of complex technology platforms across e-commerce, retail, energy, and enterprise supply-chain environments. He has progressed from hands-on automation and validation into senior technical leadership and subject-matter-expert responsibilities.

A recurring theme in his work is the validation of business transactions that cross multiple applications, services, databases, integration layers, and enterprise platforms. This has shaped his focus on end-to-end data integrity, transaction reconciliation, risk-based testing, observability, and detection of failures that conventional UI testing may not reveal.

Building on industry experience, his current professional and research interests include AI-assisted software testing, intelligent automation, autonomous validation, distributed-system reliability, end-to-end reconciliation, and quality engineering for cloud-native and enterprise systems.
